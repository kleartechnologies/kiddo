02 — Construction

The rules that make them a family

Proportion grid
One mass, not a head on a body. Total height is two body-widths; the eye line sits at 45% of full height on every character. Limbs are short enough that the silhouette stays a circle.
Shared primitives
Four solid shapes and four face parts build all five characters. Arms and legs are literally the same two objects across the cast — only rotation changes.
RULE 01
One hue per character
Every lighter form — belly, inner ear, muzzle, tail tip — is the same hue with white laid over it at a fixed opacity. No second colour, no gradients. Change one hex and the character is fully recoloured for a content pack.
RULE 02
Faces are always ink
Eyes, mouths and noses use a single warm near-black (#2E2A32) at every size. Only the blush is warm. This is what keeps expressions readable on a game card and inside a 24px sticker.
RULE 03
No outlines, no detail below 8px
Flat fills only. Any feature that would disappear at icon scale is not drawn at all — which is why there is no fur, no fingers, no rendering and no texture anywhere in the cast.

07 — Rig

Built to be animated

Seven layers, four pivots. Everything in the pose library above is one of these parts rotated around a fixed point.

Idle loop
Core scales 1.00 → 1.02 on a 2.4s ease-in-out, ears lag by 120ms. Nothing else moves. This is the default state on every screen a character appears on.
Blink
Eyes scale to 0.1 on the y axis for 90ms at a random 3–7s interval. Because the face is its own layer, the blink is one transform and never touches the body.
Reactions
Swap the face layer and rotate the limbs, 220ms with a slight overshoot. No squash on the silhouette so the character never feels rubbery.
08 — Guardrails

What keeps this premium

Always
Flat fills, one hue per character, white overlays for every lighter form.
Eyes at 45% height, always the same ink, always with a single offset highlight.
Silhouette readable as a solid black shape before any face is added.
Encouraging faces on failure states. Characters never scold, frown or look disappointed.
Generous negative space around a character — at least 12% of the frame on every side.
Never
Gradients, drop shadows on the characters, glow, outlines or texture.
A sixth hue, a neon tint, or two saturated colours on one character.
Pupils drawn inside the eye, eyebrows on KIDDO except in thinking and surprised.
Baby-talk copy, exclamation stacks, or more than one character celebrating at once.
Fingers, teeth, tongues outside the two open-mouth faces, or any detail under 8px.
