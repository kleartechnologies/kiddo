Compact spec · v1 proposal

KIDDO visual system

What changes, what does not, and in what order. Nothing here asks for a new engine, a new challenge kind, a new animation vocabulary or a new dependency. Companion files: KIDDO Character Family and KIDDO Child Experience.

1 · Principles

01

Show the thing, then name it

A four-year-old who cannot read the prompt must still know what to do. Illustration carries meaning first; the word confirms it.

02

Everything stands somewhere

No object floats on a card. A duck sits by water, apples grow on grass, numbers are signs in the ground. The board is a place.

03

Motion answers the child

Nothing loops, nothing plays because time passed, nothing moves to say “wrong”. Movement is a consequence of a tap and it goes where the tap said.

04

Praise finishing, never score

No XP, coins, points, scores, streaks, lives, leaderboards or timers. Progress is a flower, an animal, a page. Never a number, never a bar.

05

Warm, never alarming

No red, no cross, no shake, no scolding face. Retry is apricot and a magnifier: keep looking. Hard is brighter, never darker.

06

Data, not screens

A world is a layer list, a character is a canon file, a celebration is a moment token. Adding one is an entry in a table, never a new component.

2 · Colour

Same six families, same names, same one-hue-per-character rule, same file (globals.css @theme). Each base gains chroma so the character reads at 48px. Every other step is re-derived from it.

Re-run the colour-blindness sweep

TELLABLE_PAIRS in accents.ts lists the five pairs that survive protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia. Raising chroma changes those distances — the five must be re-measured, not assumed. Every colour-answer board draws from that list, and its tests check it.

Environment shades are new, and scene-only

Scenes need depth the six hues cannot give: ridge #B7D9AE, canopy #6BA45C, wood #C98A4E, soil #4A4038. These are never UI surfaces, never text, never a seventh accent family.

3 · Type, shape, motion

Type — unchanged

Fredoka display, Nunito sans. Two families, no third. Child-facing minimum 16px; a prompt is 20–24px; a celebration title 36–42px. Numerals on a sign are 44px+.

Shape — unchanged

tile 20px · card 28px · hero 40px. Buttons are full pills with a solid bottom edge — the toy press. Shadows stay warm, never grey.

Motion — unchanged

The eight Magic Motions and no ninth: pop bounce float slide walk grow sparkle celebrate. Only at the four engine moments: arrive, right, joined, partner.

4 · Illustration style

Rules that hold

  • Flat shapes with two-stop soft shading. No outlines, no rim light, no third stop.
  • One light direction for the whole product: upper-left, 34% / 24%.
  • Silhouette first — every drawing must be identifiable at 48px in one colour.
  • Faces are ink. Only cheeks are warm.
  • No brown, no grey, no red as a UI colour. Wood and soil are scene-only.
  • Every illustration is aria-hidden. The word lives in the accessible name.

The one rule that changes

canon.ts RULE 01 forbids gradients and makes every lighter form a white overlay. The reference is soft-shaded, and matching it means a two-stop radial per hue and a real cream fill for bellies and muzzles.

Cost of keeping RULE 01: the characters stay flat and read closer to today's. Cost of changing it: one paragraph in canon.ts, one <defs> block per character, and slightly larger SVG. This is a decision to take deliberately, not by accident.

Scenes, not stickers — the promotion rule stays

art.ts makes a drawing a promotion: an item without one still draws as a glyph, so a pack can write a fact about an octopus on day one. That stays. What changes is the standard for a drawing once it exists — a place.* is a small composition with a ground line (already true), and an animal.* gets a pose and a contact shadow so it can stand on that ground rather than hover over it.

6 · Per-mechanic visual systems

MechanicReads asStatesRule that must not break
Activity intro The world before the round starts: ground band, 2–3 props, the resident friend standing in it, and the first board's prompt items already there. Fresh (no picker) · returning (tier picker) · reduced-motion (identical, still). The glimpse comes from introPreviewOf, which returns prompt items and never options. Previewing options previews the answer.
Connect Characters finding their partner. Left things stand on the land, right things are drawn where they belong; the join is a dotted trail. idle · invited (static dashed tide on every unclaimed destination, from first paint) · selected (honey ring) · wrong (apricot, 1.2s, selection clears) · joined (travel, nestle, check, sparkle, paw print left behind). Travel is destination − origin on both axes, read from the node actually tapped. No code path moves x without y.
Choice Objects and creatures standing on a patch of their own world. Wide tiles on a phone, square where the subject is a picture. idle · correct (yes-soft + tick) · wrong (retry-soft + magnifier + one settle) · tried (quieter, still tappable) · dimmed. A tile never disappears. Removing one moves the board and loses the child's place.
Counting Real things in a place — apples on grass, flowers in a bed, shells by water — and numbers as signs planted in the near ground. Objects pop in one at a time (existing PromptDisplay); the right sign sparkles. The post is decoration below the sign and never part of the touch target.
Order (spec only) Arranging things along something real — stepping stones across water, a shelf, a washing line — rather than numbered dashed boxes. Empty slot (soft depression in the ground) · armed next slot (honey ring) · placed (settles with a small bounce) · complete (the row reads as one picture). Slot order, keyboard order and the sr-only description stay OrderStage's. The scenery may not renumber anything.
Match (spec only) Discovering a relationship: two things meet in the middle and stay together, on a surface that belongs to the world. face-down / unmatched (world-appropriate back) · lifted · matched (pair drifts together, single sparkle) · settled. The invited state that MatchStage already has, applied statically before the first touch — same fix as Connect.

7 · Difficulty & celebration

Easy · Medium · Hard

TierMarkScene treatment
EasyCheckThe world at rest. Fewest props, widest spacing, biggest targets.
MediumArrowRightMore world: a second tree, a few butterflies, a fuller ground. Same sky.
HardLock when lockedFullest scene, most life, most colour. Brighter, never darker; no storm, no dusk, no red.

Marks are lucide icons at size-4, strokeWidth 2.5 — the set TierPicker.tsx already imports. No emoji anywhere in the child-facing chrome; the whole point of the brief is to get away from symbols standing in for pictures.

State is never colour alone. A locked tier is a real focusable button that announces “Hard. Locked.” and does nothing — a locked door still has a handle.

MomentLightWhat else

worldgolden ring + long raysThe world itself changes behind: the garden blooms, its resident appears, and the title is the world's own line — “Your whole garden is in bloom!”

The lift stays the only thing that moves, and it is the same size at every moment. All four are distinguishable as still frames, so the hierarchy survives prefers-reduced-motion intact.

8 · Responsive & accessibility

ViewportRule
360 × 640The floor. Scene compresses to a ground band plus one prop; the board never scrolls; celebration fits without scroll. Choice tiles go wide, one per row.
390 × 844Primary. Full scene, board pinned to the bottom edge, host and bubble above it.
768 × 1024Scene gains depth layers and side props; the board centres at its natural size rather than stretching. Connect columns widen, rows keep their spacing.
1440 × 900Short and wide: the scene grows horizontally, never vertically. Content column capped; props fill the margins so the world does not become a strip.

Scene props are positioned in percentages against the board, so a layer list works at every size without a breakpoint per world.

Preserved exactly

  • Touch targets 56px+ (tap-target), never reduced for a scene.
  • aria-disabled, never the disabled attribute — focus must not jump.
  • One sr-only role="status" for the whole round; it never remounts with the board.
  • Every illustration aria-hidden; the name lives in aria-label / spokenOf.
  • State never colour alone: tick, magnifier, padlock, arrow, paw print.
  • One focus ring — 3px ink, 3px offset — over every scene.
  • MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"; every motion has a settled state, and the CSS reduce block already covers plain transitions.
  • Hydration-safe: scenes are deterministic, never Math.random.

Cultural safety — authoritative, untouched

vocabulary.ts and tests/safety.test.ts govern. No pig, piglet, oink, pork, bacon or ham — in a word, a sound, a label or a drawing. No farm machinery as an animal home. Every new illustration goes through the same sweep before it ships.
